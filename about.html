About Me

I'm an interdisciplinary engineer based in Cambridge, MA, with experience across software development, materials science, digital fabrication, and technical design. I enjoy working with a wide range of tools — from Python, SQL, and cloud platforms to CAD software, electronics, prototyping, and material-based processes.

My work often combines physical and digital systems, including interactive installations, visualization tools, web applications, fabrication projects, and experimental objects. I'm especially interested in choosing the right tools for each idea and moving between engineering, design, and artistic practice.
